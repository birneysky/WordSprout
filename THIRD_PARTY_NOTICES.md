# Third-party notices

## Mandarin pronunciation recordings

Most files in `public/audio/pronunciations/` are adapted from the
[audio-cmn](https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn) Mandarin audio collection.
The syllable recordings are attributed to Chen Wang; the collection is
maintained by Hugo Lopez and distributed under a Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike license (CC BY-SA).

The imported MP3 recordings were transcoded to AAC/M4A and loudness-normalized
for offline playback in 字芽. The exact imported and retained files are listed
in `tools/pronunciation-source-manifest.json`.
